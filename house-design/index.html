First Floor

~1,536 sf · entire east wall = open living/glass · bedrooms in W corners · level driveway entry W
NW corner: ADA bedroom (15'×12') with private accessible bath next door — roll-in shower, no thresholds, level driveway access through the mudroom. SW corner: Master suite (15'×10') + walk-in closet (7'×5') + luxury bath (15'×7') with multi-head shower and soaking tub. Mudroom/entry/laundry runs along the rear (west) wall between the two bedrooms — that's where the level driveway meets the house. Eastern strip (18'×32'): open living/dining/kitchen with the 2-story east glass wall. Kitchen counter on N wall, island parallel, dining center, living south end. Stairs clustered in the central spine: stair down to basement just east of the mudroom, stair up to loft directly above. No first-floor fireplace — moved to basement office per your spec.

Basement (Walkout)

east wall = full-width walkout · SE office (fireplace S wall) · NE guest · W half = mech / pantry / bath
NE corner: Guest bedroom (18'×13') with east window/door for daylight, closet built-in. SE corner: Office/library (18'×12') with the fieldstone fireplace built into the south wall, east walkout for daylight, acoustic-treated walls for video calls, built-in bookshelves on east + west walls, window seat on east. Walkout zone between them: 18'×7' family/media space with patio doors east — connects guest ↔ office ↔ patio. Western half: Mechanical room NW (radiant manifold, water heater, panel, future battery), pantry sectioned off just south of mech (15'×7'), full bath, storage along the south wall. Earth-bermed on W/N/S sides, walkout grade on E.

Loft / Mezzanine

~384 sf · directly above east-strip living · full N–S width (32') · 12' deep E–W
Loft sits directly above the eastern living strip, taking the full N–S length of the east wall as you specified. 12' deep (E–W) leaves the eastern 6' open-to-below so the 2-story east glass still reads as a full-height window wall — you walk under the loft and the view opens up overhead. I sketched it as three zones (yoga/movement north, reading nook against the glass center, study/meditation south) so the brief's "yoga vs library — TBD" doesn't have to be a binary choice. 384 sf is enough to host all three. Stair lands at the central west edge, matching the first-floor stair below.

Open questions for the next pass

  • ADA bath: I included a private accessible bath next to the NW bedroom. Want it private (drawn) or shared with a hall bath instead?
  • Kitchen flip: Counter on N wall, island parallel. Could swap to S end of the open zone if you want the living near the N wall instead.
  • Master bath/closet swap: Closet 7'×5' adjacent to bedroom, bath along south wall. Could swap their positions.
  • First-floor fireplace gone: Removed per spec (moved to office). Sanity check — the firelight in the main living space is a real architectural gesture, and losing it changes the character of evenings in the living room. Worth keeping a second fireplace upstairs?
  • Stairs: Both clustered in the central spine for short circulation. Alternative: stair up at one end of the house, stair down at the other. More space-efficient but more walking.
  • Loft access: Single straight-run stair drawn. Could be open spiral, switchback, or floating-tread design — affects how dramatic the entry to the loft feels.
